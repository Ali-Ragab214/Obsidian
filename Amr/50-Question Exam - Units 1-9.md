# 📝 50-Question Exam — Units 1–9 (الامتحان الشامل)

> [!info] تعليمات (Instructions)
> - Answer **all 50 questions**. حلّ كل الأسئلة من غير ما تبص على الإجابات.
> - Every question is a Multiple Choice question (اختيار من متعدد). لكل سؤال إجابة واحدة صح.
> - **Questions are in random order** — الأسئلة من الوحدات متربطة بشكل عشوائي، تحدي حقيقي 🎲
> - Units 1–9 are all included. كل الوحدات موجودة (من 1 لـ 9).
> - Check your answers in [[#✅ Answer Key|Answer Key]] at the end (مترتبة برقم السؤال، ومعاها رقم الوحدة).

---

## Part 1: Questions 1–25

**1.** She is from the Netherlands. She is ___.
- a) Netherlandish
- b) Dutch
- c) Hollandian
- d) Belgian

**2.** At 8 PM, you greet someone by saying:
- a) Good morning
- b) Good afternoon
- c) Good evening
- d) Good night

**3.** "How can I get to the mall?" — "You can ___ the bus."
- a) drive
- b) take
- c) go
- d) fly

**4.** A person who walks with a visitor to guide them is called:
- a) a passenger
- b) an escort
- c) a driver
- d) a colleague

**5.** The sign telling drivers to allow other traffic on a bigger road to go first is:
- a) STOP
- b) YIELD
- c) ONE WAY
- d) PARKING

**6.** ___ is she? — She's my sister. (سؤال عن شخص)
- a) What
- b) Who
- c) Where
- d) When

**7.** Which sentence is a **negative imperative** (أمر منفي)?
- a) Turn right.
- b) Please sit down.
- c) Don't worry.
- d) Open the door.

**8.** "We ___ hurry. We have plenty of time." (مش لازم نستعجل)
- a) must not
- b) shouldn't
- c) don't have to
- d) must

**9.** A police officer specializing in investigating and solving crimes is a ___.
- a) dispatcher
- b) detective
- c) sergeant
- d) chief

**10.** A person who is friendly and likes meeting new people is:
- a) shy
- b) outgoing
- c) quiet
- d) serious

**11.** A person from Algeria is ___.
- a) Algerian
- b) Algeria
- c) Algerianic
- d) Algerish

**12.** The word **"assist"** means:
- a) يساعد
- b) يُحيّي
- c) يفتّش
- d) يوقّع

**13.** Which train is faster but more expensive?
- a) regular train
- b) express train
- c) local train
- d) slow train

**14.** A blue square with the letter **P** means:
- a) Parking
- b) Police
- c) Pharmacy
- d) Pedestrian crossing

**15.** Where do you pick up your bags after landing? (استلام الشنط)
- a) Gate
- b) Check-in
- c) Baggage claim
- d) Terminal

**16.** "You ___ park here." (ممنوع تركن هنا)
- a) don't have to
- b) must not
- c) shouldn't
- d) don't

**17.** On a form, **"Mrs."** is used for:
- a) any man
- b) a married woman
- c) an unmarried woman
- d) any woman

**18.** An emergency medical professional who gives advanced prehospital care is a ___.
- a) doctor
- b) nurse
- c) paramedic
- d) specialist

**19.** China → Chinese. Japan → ___.
- a) Japanian
- b) Japanese
- c) Japanish
- d) Japan

**20.** "How do you do?" is usually answered with:
- a) I'm fine, thanks.
- b) Not much.
- c) How do you do?
- d) Long time no see.

**21.** She ___ the visitor's credentials before granting access. (Present Simple مع she)
- a) verify
- b) verifying
- c) verifies
- d) is verify

**22.** Tomorrow I ___ to New York. (خطة محددة — Present Continuous)
- a) take
- b) am taking
- c) takes
- d) took

**23.** "Look at ___ speed limit signs over there." (جمع + بعيد)
- a) this
- b) that
- c) these
- d) those

**24.** Which is the **most polite** request (أرقى طلب مؤدب)?
- a) Open the door.
- b) Can you open the door?
- c) Could you please open the door?
- d) You open the door.

**25.** Which contraction is **wrong** (غلط)?
- a) What's her name?
- b) How old's he?
- c) Where's he from?
- d) How's Anna?

## Part 2: Questions 26–50

**26.** On John Doe's boarding pass, the flight **departs** at:
- a) 11:35 AM
- b) 12:30 PM
- c) 10:30 AM
- d) 2:30 PM

**27.** "May I see your appointment ___ and identification, please?"
- a) confirmation
- b) embassy
- c) detector
- d) belongings

**28.** Choose the correct negative sentence (صيغة النفي الصح):
- a) He not is from Japan.
- b) He isn't from Japan.
- c) He doesn't from Japan.
- d) He no is from Japan.

**29.** In the dialogue, the train departs from Platform ___ at 10:30 AM.
- a) 1
- b) 3
- c) 5
- d) 15

**30.** The security officer records visitor details in the ___.
- a) logbook
- b) dictionary
- c) mailbox
- d) drawer

**31.** The sterile room where surgeries are performed is the ___.
- a) ICU
- b) Ward
- c) Operating Room (OR)
- d) Pharmacy

**32.** "What do you do?" means:
- a) What's your hobby?
- b) Where do you live?
- c) What's your job?
- d) How are you?

**33.** "Speed Limit 30 km/h" means:
- a) You must drive at least 30.
- b) You can't drive faster than 30.
- c) The road is 30 km long.
- d) You can drive any speed.

**34.** CCTV cameras mean:
- a) كاميرات مراقبة
- b) أجهزة كشف المعادن
- c) زجاج مضاد للرصاص
- d) أزرار الطوارئ

**35.** At security, the officer says: "Please step through the metal ___."
- a) detector
- b) elevator
- c) scanner belt
- d) terminal

**36.** You meet the CEO of a company for the first time in a formal meeting. The best greeting is:
- a) Yo!
- b) How do you do?
- c) Sup?
- d) What's up?

**37.** Is Ronaldo Spanish? — No, he isn't. He's ___.
- a) Brazilian
- b) Argentine
- c) Portuguese
- d) Italian

**38.** "Visitors ___ wait for an escort." (إلزام بسبب القواعد)
- a) can
- b) have to
- c) should not
- d) must not

**39.** My bus ___ at 11:30 every day. (جدول ثابت — Present Simple)
- a) leaves
- b) is leaving
- c) leave
- d) leaving

**40.** Giving advice (نصيحة/رأي): "You ___ install CCTV cameras at all entry points."
- a) should
- b) must not
- c) don't have to
- d) can't

**41.** "What's up?" The best informal response is:
- a) How do you do?
- b) Not much, you?
- c) Goodbye.
- d) See you later.

**42.** "___ sign tells drivers to stop at the intersection." (مفرد + قريب)
- a) This
- b) That
- c) These
- d) Those

**43.** Which short answer is **correct**? (الإجابة القصيرة الصح)
- a) Yes, he's.
- b) Yes, he is.
- c) Yes, is he.
- d) Yes, he be.

**44.** On John Doe's boarding pass, the **gate** is:
- a) B12
- b) E39
- c) 15F
- d) A3

**45.** In the dialogue, Sara loves traveling, and John is into:
- a) reading and writing
- b) hiking and photography
- c) swimming and diving
- d) cooking and baking

**46.** "Go straight ahead and the post office will be ___ your left."
- a) on
- b) at
- c) in
- d) to

**47.** A doctor who performs operations is a ___.
- a) radiologist
- b) surgeon
- c) paramedic
- d) receptionist

**48.** General obligation (التزام عام من برّه): "Visitors ___ to pass through the metal detectors before entering the building."
- a) must
- b) have
- c) should
- d) can

**49.** The illegal entry into a building with intent to steal is ___.
- a) theft
- b) vandalism
- c) burglary
- d) arson

**50.** The visitor's office is ___ the second floor.
- a) at
- b) in
- c) on
- d) to
